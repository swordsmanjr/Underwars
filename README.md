# | THE UNDERWARS |

Defend your base, dig your own paths underground and lead your team to victory! Every kill gives you 10 Ruby's, and ruby's are used to buy even better items to defend with.

# | FUNCTIONALITYS |

**Inventory**

1. Shovel | Used to dig your way to victory!
2. Block | Purchased from Kyle's Blocks shop, you can use them to block enemy's or refill paths.
3. Gun | A weapon of your choice bought from Lucy's Ruby shop!

**Modes**

1. Capture the Flag | Dig to the enemy's base, and steal their flag! First team to 5 wins.
2. Normal | Defend your base! Every kill is 1 point, team with most points wins!
